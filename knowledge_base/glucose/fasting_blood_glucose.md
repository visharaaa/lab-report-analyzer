# Fasting Blood Glucose

## What it is

Blood glucose, also called blood sugar, is the main type of sugar found in the blood. Glucose provides energy for the body's cells.

## What the test measures

A fasting blood glucose test measures the amount of glucose in the blood after a period of fasting.

The test is commonly used to assess blood glucose levels and to help screen for or monitor conditions involving blood glucose regulation, including diabetes.

## Why it may be tested

A fasting blood glucose test may be performed as part of routine health screening, when diabetes or prediabetes is suspected, or to monitor blood glucose levels in people who have already been diagnosed with a blood glucose disorder.

## When the result is low

A low blood glucose level is called hypoglycemia.

Low blood glucose can occur for several reasons, including certain medicines, not eating enough, increased physical activity, or some medical conditions.

The significance of a low result depends on the glucose level, symptoms, medications, and individual circumstances.

## When the result is high

A high blood glucose level is called hyperglycemia.

High blood glucose can occur when the body does not produce enough insulin, does not respond effectively to insulin, or in other situations that affect glucose regulation.

Persistently elevated blood glucose can be associated with prediabetes or diabetes, but a single result does not by itself establish a diagnosis.

## Important context

The interpretation of fasting blood glucose depends on how long the person has fasted, the laboratory's reference range, and the clinical context.

Different tests used to assess glucose regulation have different interpretation criteria. Fasting blood glucose should not be interpreted using the same thresholds as random blood glucose or HbA1c.

A fasting blood glucose result should be considered alongside other relevant laboratory results and clinical information.

The reference range printed on the patient's laboratory report should be used when determining whether the result is low, normal, or high within this application.

## Safety note

A single fasting blood glucose result outside the laboratory's reference range does not by itself establish a diagnosis.

This knowledge base provides general educational information. The system should not diagnose diabetes, prediabetes, hypoglycemia, or another medical condition based on a fasting blood glucose result alone.
