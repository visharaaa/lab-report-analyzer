# Lymphocytes

## What it is

Lymphocytes are a type of white blood cell that plays an important role in the immune system. They help the body recognize and respond to infections and other foreign substances.

The main types of lymphocytes are B cells and T cells.

## What the test measures

A lymphocyte result indicates the amount or proportion of lymphocytes in the blood.

In a blood differential, lymphocytes may be reported as a percentage of total white blood cells or as an absolute lymphocyte count.

## Why it may be tested

Lymphocytes may be measured as part of a CBC with differential to help evaluate infections, immune system activity, and conditions affecting blood cells or the immune system.

## When the result is low

A low lymphocyte count is called lymphocytopenia or lymphopenia.

It can occur with certain infections, immune system disorders, nutritional deficiencies, some medicines or medical treatments, and conditions affecting blood cell production.

A low result alone does not identify the underlying cause.

## When the result is high

A high lymphocyte count is called lymphocytosis.

It can occur in response to certain infections and other immune responses. Some blood disorders can also be associated with increased lymphocyte counts.

A high result alone does not identify the underlying cause.

## Important context

Lymphocytes may be reported as a percentage or as an absolute count. These measurements should not be treated as interchangeable.

A lymphocyte percentage can change because the proportions of other white blood cell types change, even when the absolute lymphocyte count remains within its reference range.

Lymphocytes should therefore be interpreted alongside the total WBC count and the other differential results.

Reference ranges vary between laboratories and may depend on the measurement method.

The reference range printed on the patient's laboratory report should be used when determining whether the result is low, normal, or high.

## Safety note

An abnormal lymphocyte result does not by itself establish an infection, immune disorder, or other diagnosis.

This knowledge base provides general educational information. The system should not diagnose a medical condition based on a lymphocyte result alone.
